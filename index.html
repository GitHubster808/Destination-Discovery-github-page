Destination Discovery

Go ahead. Turn on the fun!

A vacation-planning app for Android. Name a trip, set the hotel and dates, slot in excursions, and text the itinerary to whoever's coming along.

What it does

  1. 01

    Plan the trip

    Name the vacation, log the hotel stay, and pick a start and end date.

    START → END
  2. 02

    Add excursions

    Slot activities into the trip window, each with its own date.

    IN-TRIP
  3. 03

    Get notified

    Turn on a reminder for a whole vacation or a single excursion.

    ON DEMAND
  4. 04

    Share the itinerary

    Send the hotel and dates straight to a contact by text.

    SMS
